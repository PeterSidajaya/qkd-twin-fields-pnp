\caption{Stage-3 representation chain.  The original source is an infinite
distribution on the Fock lattice.  The exact rescaling
\(u_X=\tau m_X\) pushes it forward to the lattice measure \(\nu_\tau\) and
carries the mean-energy constraint from
\(\mathbb E[M_X]\le\bar n/(2\tau)\) to
\(\int u_X\,d\nu_\tau\le\bar n/2\).  Dropping the lattice support gives the
continuum outer relaxation \(\nu\).  The final finite variables are cell
masses \(q_j=\nu(C_j)\), not point masses at the representatives \(c_j\);
the quantities \(\delta_{st,j}\) account for all possible kernel variation
inside each cell.}
